Video Paylas

Personal video scheduling tool

Video Paylas is a personal automation tool used only by its owner. It reads a private content plan from the owner's Google Sheet, takes the owner's own video files from the owner's Google Drive, and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts at the times the owner schedules.

The tool does not have public sign-up, does not serve other users, and does not collect or store data about anyone except its owner.

How it works

1. The owner adds video files to a private Google Drive folder and schedules them in a private Google Sheet.

2. A scheduled job runs in the owner's private GitHub repository, downloads the due video, and publishes it through the official platform APIs.

3. The result (published link or error) is written back to the owner's private sheet.

Platform connections

The tool uses official APIs: YouTube Data API, Instagram Graph API and TikTok Content Posting API. Access is authorized once by the owner through each platform's login screen. Access tokens are stored in the owner's private Google Sheet and in private repository secrets, and are never shared with third parties.
