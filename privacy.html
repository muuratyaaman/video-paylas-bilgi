Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Video Paylas ("the app") is a personal automation tool used only by its owner. This policy explains what data the app handles and how it is stored.

1. Who uses the app

The app has no public sign-up. Only its owner can use it, through the owner's own accounts. It does not offer services to other people.

2. Data the app handles

  • Video files that the owner places in the owner's own Google Drive folder.
  • Video titles, descriptions and schedule times that the owner enters in the owner's own Google Sheet.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by YouTube, Instagram and TikTok after the owner authorizes the app.
  • Basic account information returned by those platforms (for example the account display name and open ID) used only to confirm which account is connected.

3. Data the app does not collect

The app does not collect data about viewers, followers or any other person. It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking pixels or cookies. It does not read private messages or content that does not belong to the owner.

4. How data is stored

Tokens are stored in the owner's private Google Sheet and in private GitHub repository secrets. The Google Sheet and the repository are accessible only to the owner. Data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties. Data is transmitted only to the official platform APIs needed to publish the owner's videos.

5. Data retention and deletion

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from the security settings of YouTube, Instagram or TikTok, and can delete the stored tokens from the private Google Sheet. Once access is revoked, the app can no longer act on that account.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The current version is always available at this address.
